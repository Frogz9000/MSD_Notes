# Pulse Oximeter

## What is a Pulse Oximeter and What Does it Do
<img title="Pulse Ox" alt="An Image of a Pulse Oximeter" src="../images/pulse_ox.jpg" width = 200px>

Imaged above is a standard finger pulse oximeter. Pulse oximeters are often abreviated to Pulse Ox and will be for the rest of this document. Most modules are attatched to the finger but there exists alternatives that can be attatched to the toes, nose, ears, or other extremities [1].

The purpose of a Pulse Ox is to non-invasively measure the heart rate and blood oxygen the user. Often time a Pulse Ox works in conjunction with a vital monitering device to alert medical personal of low blood oxygen levels to allow for preventative care.
